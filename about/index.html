Mingjing Xu is a Computer Science PhD student at Swansea University working on efficient optimization, trustworthy AI, multimodal representation learning, and edge intelligence. He is first author of PSMGD, an AAAI 2025 paper on fast stochastic multi-objective optimization, and co-first author of recent ICLR 2026 and AAAI 2026 work on neural network inheritance and tactile-language-vision alignment. His earlier work spans practical adversarial attacks on WiFi sensing at ACM MobiCom 2024, magnetic-guided medical robotics at IEEE ICRA 2024, embedded firmware for IoT radio systems at Jabil, and Android/Kotlin teaching at Temple University.
